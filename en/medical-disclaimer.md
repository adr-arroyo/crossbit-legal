# CrossBit Medical Disclaimer

**Last updated:** 1 September 2026.

CrossBit is a tool for tracking physical activity, nutrition and wellbeing. This
notice forms part of the [Terms and Conditions](/en/terms.html).

## 1. Not medical advice

The information provided by CrossBit—including AI coach responses, analyses,
workout recommendations, nutrition plans and readiness metrics—is for informational
and educational purposes only. It is **not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or
professional nutritional advice**, and it does not replace consultation with a
doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian or other qualified healthcare professional.

## 2. Consult a professional before starting

Before starting or changing an exercise programme or eating plan, especially if you
have a medical condition, are pregnant, take medication, are recovering from an
injury or have another risk factor, **consult a healthcare professional**. Do not
ignore or delay medical care because of anything you read in CrossBit.

## 3. AI can be wrong

The AI coach generates content automatically and **may provide information that is
inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable for your personal situation**. Check any
health-related decision with a professional. Do not use CrossBit to diagnose or
treat any condition.

## 4. Emergencies

CrossBit **is not an emergency service**. If you believe you are experiencing a
medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately, including 112
in the European Union where appropriate.

## 5. Physical-activity risk

Physical exercise, particularly high-intensity training such as CrossFit, carries
inherent risks of injury. You are responsible for training within your limits, using
proper technique and obtaining any supervision you need. Adrián Arroyo Pérez is not
responsible for injuries or damage arising from use of information in the app.

## 6. Metrics

Health and activity metrics shown in CrossBit may come from manual input or app
estimates and **are not clinical-grade measurements**. Do not use them as the basis
for medical decisions.
